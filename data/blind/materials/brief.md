# International Track — Topics & Rules (official Attachment 2, transcribed 2026-10-16)

> Source: `international-topics-rules-en.docx` provided by the user (Attachment 2 on fintechathon.g-ican.com). All design must align with this. **Chosen topic: B (Wealth Advisory Agent).**

## 1. Theme
The theme of this year's International Track is "AI as a Financial Participant": each team designs and builds an AI agent capable of completing real tasks in a financial scenario. Choose one of the six topics below.

## 2. Topics (Choose One of Six)
- Topic A | Personal Finance Assistant: An intelligent assistant for individual users, capable of smart budgeting, bill analysis, and spending insights, delivering actionable recommendations based on the user's income and expenses.
- **Topic B | Wealth Advisory Agent: An agent that mirrors a professional wealth-advisory workflow — product comparison, client risk profiling, and advisory dialogue — producing compliant and explainable asset-allocation suggestions.**
- Topic C | Cross-border & Student Finance Assistant: remittances, tuition payments, credit building, multi-currency management.
- Topic D | Insurance Assistant: policy Q&A, coverage-gap analysis, claims guidance.
- Topic E | SME Finance Copilot: financing-option matching, cash-flow management and alerts, business financial analysis.
- Topic F | Agent Wallet — Autonomous On-chain Wallet; 0–5 point challenge bonus.

## 3. Unified Requirements and Scoring
All six topics share the same core capability requirements and scoring criteria; choosing the on-chain topic does not affect scoring:
- Task completion: 40%
- Security & compliance: 30%
- Innovation & interaction: 30%
- Challenge bonus (on-chain exploration): 0–5 points

Official website judging detail (from the site's International AI track criteria, found during research):
- Task Completion 40%: completion of scripted tasks in a simulated environment for non-on-chain topics.
- Security & Compliance 30%: defence against induced transfers, data extraction, privilege escalation and prompt injection; serious security failures trigger substantial deductions.
- Innovation & Interaction 30%: scenario novelty, real-world value, dialogue efficiency, clarification, correction and fallback capability.

## 4. Eligibility and Teaming
1. Full-time students at overseas universities and research institutions (outside China incl. HK, Macao, Taiwan).
2. Teams of 2–5 students; one team per person across Domestic and International tracks.
3. English is the official language; submissions, presentations, and reviews are all in English.
4. At least 60% of a team's members should be non-Chinese nationals (rounded up to whole persons per the website). Track assignment by the Organizing Committee.

## 5. Submission
Registration and submission share the same deadline: **October 20, 2026, 23:59 Beijing Time (UTC+8)** = 15:59 UTC. Checklist:
1. Technical documentation (system architecture, core algorithm description, security design)
2. Presentation deck (final presentation ≤ 10 minutes)
3. Demo video (≤ 5 minutes; the website lists it as required)
4. Source code (README, GitHub repository link, deployment instructions, test cases)
5. Security self-assessment report (permission-tier implementation and known-risk list)
6. Execution evidence (non-on-chain topics: simulated-account/sandbox operation logs and demo video)

Submissions may be updated any time before the deadline; the latest version prevails. Registration is by email (form xlsx) — see research notes in DECISIONS/HANDOFF.

## 6. Review and Grand Final
Late October online review (WeBank experts + scholars); finalists by Oct 31; Nov 20 check-in Shenzhen; Nov 21–23 final (closed-door English defence) & awards. At least one member must attend in person.

## 7. Prizes, IP
Pool RMB 250,000 (100k / 80k / 50k / Best Innovation 20k / Excellence awards). IP remains with contestants; organisers get non-exclusive non-commercial publicity rights; third-party components must be properly licensed.
